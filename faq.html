Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral from my GP?

No. You can book directly with us, including for ACC injuries.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us 24 hours notice. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged a $40 fee.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing that lets us see the area being treated. Shorts for knee and hip problems.

Do you treat children?

Yes, we treat children aged 8 and over. A parent or guardian must attend.

Can I claim on insurance?

Yes. We are Southern Cross Easy-claim providers, and most other insurers reimburse our receipts.
